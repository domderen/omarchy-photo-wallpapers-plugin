# Family campaign provenance

The people in these eight family wallpapers are fictional. The photographs and
blank MacBook-style device scenes were created with built-in image_gen; no
personal photo or real likeness reference was supplied. Exact prompts are in
PROMPTS.md. The installed plugin does not generate photos. Apple/MacBook names
identify illustrated hardware and do not imply endorsement.

The hero and marketplace preview use **Carry me home**, a candid father/daughter
beach moment, in the silver studio frame. It was selected at full size and as a
620-pixel thumbnail for readable faces, warm family emotion and a large clear
display. The preview is a WebP encoding of the accepted hero composition; its
screen, framing and subjects are unchanged. No further image generation was
needed. The full private review retains the owner's other approved options.

Twenty-nine complete real 1920×1200 Omarchy screenshots are included. On
1 November, the unmodified 0.5.0 native component was captured in overview,
theme/rotation-controls and photo-privacy views for all eight style/mode
combinations (24 frames). Eight refreshed overviews replace the screens in the
selected device composites. The approved hero and four VS Code/Foot or
Chromium/Files layouts retain the 30 October campaign. Thirteen compositions
remain selected; the other 16 current native views are supplied as raw screens.
Original screens are under screens/.

The native component connects to a real isolated 0.5.0 Rust demo daemon and is
honestly signed out of Apple with empty credentials. Its optional models are
not installed and both privacy flags are off; the screenshots do not pretend
to demonstrate screening accuracy or an Active protection session. Fixed Demo
coordinates, fictional albums, source selection and a 3600-second interval are
visual examples, not fresh sign-in or real sunrise evidence. captures.json
records actual dimensions, hashes, views and version provenance.
The app prepared all wallpapers through its actual Rust analysis/framing/render
pipeline without reducing quality gates. Inputs are 1586×992; the actual capture
mode was 1920×1200 at scale 1.25, within its normal enlargement allowance. These
are not advertised as native 4K source photographs. Matugen 4.2.0 and Omarchy's
public theme hooks produced the themes. Every accepted desktop background was
checked against the prepared render, and capture daemons performed zero new
image-analysis jobs. Initial palette generation is separate work.

VS Code used a clean profile with the generated Omarchy theme. Chromium showed
local fictional demo content; Files used isolated settings and its native
light/dark appearance. Tint coverage follows the actual installed templates and
application support. Window sizes/positions vary across the four app examples.

Each screen was deterministically perspective-mapped into a generated blank
display: complete capture, 2× projective bicubic resampling followed by Lanczos
reduction, no generative UI edits. composition.json records source, device and
output hashes plus the four screen corners. Device bases used by these selected
compositions are included under devices/. Generation, captures and prompts omit
private working paths. Gallery.html is a static, local view with links to every
original screen and full-size composition, without scripts or network calls.

The real production output, workspace, wallpaper and non-revision preferences
were verified restored after capture; dedicated QA services were stopped.
Private Photos originals, personal screenshots, People membership, account
sessions and pairing data are excluded from this set and its public provenance.
The original development repository and history remain private.
